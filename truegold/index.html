Truegold Planner

Enter the Truegold level of your buildings and the level you want. The planner adds every upgrade your goal requires, then gives the Truegold, Tempered Truegold and time it takes, and the cheapest way to refine the Tempered Truegold.

Only during KvK preparation week. Please upgrade only during the KvK preparation phase week, so everything you spend also scores KvK points.

Start here

  1. Pick your goal, for example 2 troop buildings to TG 8, and set your buildings' current levels.
  2. Add your Truegold, speedups and construction speed.
  3. Set the dates: when Super Refinement opens and, if you have one, the day you want to be ready. Then follow the plan.

3Dates

Steps from TG 5.1 on also cost Tempered Truegold, made with Super Refinement. The planner refines only what your goal needs, at the lowest price.

How Super Refinement works
  • It's in the Truegold Crucible, once your Town Center is TG 5 and your kingdom reaches the Truegold 8 stage.
  • Each refinement turns Truegold into a random amount of Tempered Truegold, up to 100 a week. The counter resets weekly; the planner takes the reset as Monday.
  • The price rises every 20 refinements in the week (table below).
  • The first refinement each day is 50% off. Best routine: a batch right after the weekly reset, then one a day on the other 6 days, so the discount lands on the expensive ones.
  • With a "Ready by" date, the planner spreads refining evenly over the weeks left, which costs less than doing it all in one week.

    How it's worked out

    Costs, build times and requirements for every step from level 30 to TG 8 come from Kingshot Optimizer's building pages; the totals match kingshot.net (29,927 Truegold and 904 Tempered Truegold for all 8 buildings to TG 8). Academy level 30, needed for Town Center 30.1, is assumed done. Building icons are also from Kingshot Optimizer.

    Build time = base time ÷ (1 + construction speed + Gray Wolf + Minister + Ground Works + KvK), then × 0.8 with Double Time, the same way as Kingshot Optimizer's building calculator. The planner applies the bonuses you pick to every step. Gray Wolf and Double Time last 5 minutes and Minister 30 minutes: activate them, then spend your construction speedups inside that window so every upgrade gets the bonus. Speedups are used for the whole goal: if they cover its total build time every upgrade is instant, otherwise each build is shortened by the same share. The schedule adds your daily Truegold every day and refines once a week (the opening week, then every Monday), from the day Super Refinement opens and your Town Center is TG 5, while your goal still needs Tempered Truegold. With a "Ready by" date it spreads refining evenly over the weeks left; the shortfall shown is against the most Tempered your Truegold can buy by then. Tempered Truegold uses the average result per refinement, so real luck will vary.

    Refinement prices and average results come from community guides (h5joy Kingshot guide); the 50% daily discount is applied to one refinement per day.
