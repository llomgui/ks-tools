Truegold Planner

Enter the Truegold level of your buildings and the level you want. The planner adds every upgrade your goal requires, then gives the Truegold, Tempered Truegold and time it takes, and the cheapest way to refine the Tempered Truegold.

Only during KvK preparation week. Please upgrade only during the KvK preparation phase week, so everything you spend also scores KvK points.

Start here

  1. Set each building's level now, and the level you want. Leave the goal as it is for buildings you don't plan to upgrade: anything your goal needs is added for you.
  2. Add your Truegold, Tempered Truegold and daily income, plus your construction speed.
  3. Pick a Super Refinement plan for Tempered Truegold, then follow the order on the right.

Steps from TG 5.1 on also cost Tempered Truegold. You make it with Super Refinement in the Truegold Crucible, which opens once your Town Center is TG 5 and your kingdom reaches the Truegold 8 stage.

  • Each refinement turns Truegold into a random amount of Tempered Truegold, up to 100 a week.
  • The price rises every 20 refinements in the week (table below), and the counter resets weekly.
  • The first refinement each day is 50% off. Best routine: a batch right after the weekly reset, then one a day on the other 6 days, so the discount lands on the expensive ones.

Order

Day 0 is today. The Town Center goes first, and other buildings fill the free builder when their requirements are met and the Truegold is there.

    How it's worked out

    Costs, build times and requirements for every step from level 30 to TG 8 come from Kingshot Optimizer's building pages; the totals match kingshot.net (29,927 Truegold and 904 Tempered Truegold for all 8 buildings to TG 8). Academy level 30, needed for Town Center 30.1, is assumed done.

    Build time is divided by (1 + construction speed). Speedups aren't counted. The schedule adds your daily Truegold every day and runs your Super Refinement plan once a week, from the day your Town Center is TG 5, while your goal still needs Tempered Truegold. Tempered Truegold uses the average result per refinement, so real luck will vary.

    Refinement prices and average results come from community guides (h5joy Kingshot guide); the 50% daily discount is applied to one refinement per day.
