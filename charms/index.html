Enter the level of your 18 charms and your Charm Guides and Charm Designs. The planner gives the upgrade order that adds the most Lethality and Health, and the KvK points it scores.

    How it's worked out

    Costs, Lethality and Health per level and Charm Score come from Kingshot Optimizer's Governor Charms reference. Every charm gives its troop type the same bonus to both Lethality and Health; which slot it's in doesn't matter. Maxing one charm (Lv 1 → 22) takes 10,880 Charm Guides and 19,200 Charm Designs and gives +99%.

    The plan buys one purchase at a time: the one that adds the most per material, where each material is priced by how scarce it is for you right now (cost ÷ amount left², like Kingshot Optimizer). Charm Designs run out first for most players, so levels that are cheap in Designs come first. "Most KvK points" picks by Charm Score instead: it often pushes a few charms very high, which scores well but isn't the best for your account.

    Exchanges cover a shortfall with the other material: 1:1 for the first 10 a week in each direction, then 2:1 for up to 500 more.

    KvK points: 70 per Charm Score on day 1 (City Construction), day 3 (Pet Training) and day 4 (Hero Development).
