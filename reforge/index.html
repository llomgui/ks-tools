Hero Gear Reforge Planner

Upgraded gear on the wrong heroes? List every hero gear piece you own. The planner keeps the best piece of each troop and slot for your three main heroes (Infantry, Cavalry, Archer), reforges the rest, and shows the best way to spend what comes back.

Only during KvK preparation week. Please upgrade and reforge only during the KvK preparation phase week, so everything you spend also scores KvK points.

Start here

  1. Open Backpack → Gear in the game and add every piece that has a +N badge or a Lv. label. Pieces with neither have nothing invested, so you can skip them.
  2. Add what is in your backpack: Forgehammers, Mythic Gear, Mithril, XP and Lucky Hero Gear Chests.
  3. Choose what you plan for: attack, garrison or a mix. Then follow the steps on the right.

      How refunds are counted

      XP and mastery are reforged separately, so you can do one, the other or both. Reforging the XP returns 100% of the Enhancement XP spent on the piece. Reforging the mastery returns 50% of its Forgehammers; the other half is lost. Untick XP or Hammers on a piece in the steps to keep that part. The piece must be unequipped first. You get one free reforge a day, and 100 gems skips the 24-hour wait.

      Mythic Gear and Mithril spent on mastery above 10, ascending and imbuements are not counted as refunded. Check the refund preview in the game before you confirm; if it shows more, add it to step 2.

      A piece can also move to another hero of the same troop type and keep all its levels, for free. So the planner keeps the strongest piece you own for each troop and slot on your main hero of that troop, then reforges the others. The upgrade order uses the same optimizer and cost tables as the Hero Gear planner.
