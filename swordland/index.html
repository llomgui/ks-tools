Swordland Showdown Planner

Plan one legion: who holds each building and where everyone else sends their marches, step by step through the hour.

Start here

  1. Players: type or paste your legion in the list below, strongest first, and set each one to Join or Substitute like in the game's Legion Combatants list.
  2. Plan each step: pick a step, click a building and choose its holder (tier A+ or A) and the garrison marches. Or drag a player's number onto a building (on a phone: tap the number, then the building).
  3. Suggest plan fills what is still empty from power: holders on the buildings, garrisons, rallies, looters and Undercellars, in every step. What you already set stays.
  4. Just looking? Load example shows a real 39-player legion with its suggested plan.
  5. Share: copy the code or link, or Copy for chat for a plain text plan.

    Swordland rules used
    • A legion has 30 players and 10 substitutes. Substitutes only get in during the first 3 minutes, if someone doesn't come.
    • The match lasts 60 minutes. The Swordshrine, Mercenary and Reformation are locked for the first 15 minutes.
    • A building is yours after 3 minutes inside (1.5 with the Bell Tower). You then get the first control bonus once, and points every minute you hold it.
    • Points (alliance, personal is half): Swordshrine 9,000 then +1,800/min, Sanctum 6,000 +1,200/min, Abbey 3,000 +600/min, Bell Tower, Mercenary, Reformation and Stables 1,200 +240/min.
    • Half of a building's points pile up on it. When the enemy takes it, the pile drops on the ground: send spare marches to loot it.
    • Stables: free teleport every 5 minutes instead of 10. Bell Tower: captures take half the time. Reformation: troop buff. Mercenary: mercenary attacks on buildings.
    • Undercellars: 8 appear in phase 3, two waves of four about 15 and 20 minutes into the phase, at random spots (the magnifying glass on the minimap shows them). Each holds 4,800 points. We send 2 gatherers to each.
    • Marches: every building held, garrison, Undercellar and rally uses one; a teleport (to loot) brings every march home.
    • Kills only give personal points: 80 per 10,000 enemy power when attacking, 40 when defending.
    • Troops are only injured. Leaving the field heals them, then you wait 12 minutes before you can enter again.
