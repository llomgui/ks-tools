KvK Preparation Planner

Enter what's in your backpack and bring in your gear, charms and Truegold plans. The planner shows what to use on each of the 5 preparation days so you reach as many milestones as possible.

Start here

  1. Bring in your plans: press Use my KS Tools pages, or paste codes from the Hero Gear (KGO:), Governor Gear (GOV:), Charms (KSC1:) or Truegold (KST1:) pages.
  2. Fill in the rest of your backpack: speedups, hero shards, pet items, troops.
  3. Follow the day plan. Keep everything until its day: nothing you use before the week starts counts.

Points per item and how the plan is made

Items that score on several days (speedups, Truegold, Mithril, hero shards…) give the same points whichever day you use them. The planner first puts single-day items on their day, then spends the flexible ones to reach each day's first milestone, then the second, then the third, so as many milestones as possible are reached. What's left goes to the day with the fewest points.
