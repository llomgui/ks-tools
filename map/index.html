Alliance Map Planner

Plan where your alliance builds around the King's Castle: HQs, flags, Hunting Traps and every member's town. The map checks the placement rules and shows your territory.

Start here

  1. Already planned on kingshot.me? Press Export Map there, then Open kingshot.me file here.
  2. Build: pick a building and click the map. HQs first, then flags to grow the territory, then Hunting Traps inside it. An alliance is ready; add more only if you plan for several.
  3. Order your players: select a trap or the castle, paste the player names strongest first and press Arrange: they fill a square around it, closest first. Drag a town onto another to swap them.
  4. Share: copy the code or link and post it in your alliance chat. Opening it loads the whole map.

Drag to move the map, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Placement rules used
  • Town 2×2. Alliance HQ, Hunting Trap and Great buildings 3×3, flag 1×1.
  • An HQ claims 15×15 tiles around it, a flag 7×7. Plain HQ only on plain, Wilderness HQ only on badland (outside the plain).
  • A flag's area must touch your territory. Traps and Great buildings must be fully on your territory.
  • One of each HQ and trap, and one Great building per alliance.
  • Nothing is built in the castle area (X/Y 594 to 605). Towns can stand in the forbidden ring (586 to 613) and the ruins (552 to 647), alliance buildings can't. Territory doesn't cover any of them.
  • Tiles already claimed stay with whoever claimed them first.

Mountains, water and outposts are not on this map: check those in the game.
