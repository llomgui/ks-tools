Hero Gear Upgrade Planner

Enter your resources and your 12 hero gear pieces. The planner ranks every possible upgrade by weighted stat gain per share of budget, the same greedy method as Kingshot Optimizer, and gives you an order to follow.

Start here

  1. Load your account. No code? Just type your numbers into the panels below. If you have a KGO code from Kingshot Optimizer, paste it here and press Import. To see how the planner works first, press Load example.
  2. Check your resources and 12 gear pieces in the panels below. Tap any piece in the results to jump to it.
  3. Pick your formation, then follow the upgrade order on the right.

    How the order is chosen

    Each piece's value is stat × (1 + 0.1 × mastery) × profile weight. Every candidate move (one mastery level, enhancement levels, an ascension, or crossing an imbuement gate together with the mastery it needs) is scored by weighted gain divided by the share of each resource it uses. Locked resources (Forgehammers, Mythic Gear, Mithril and XP in Red gear) count 10× more than XP in Mythic gear, which can be reforged. The best move is applied and the scan repeats. The whole plan runs twice, once chasing milestone bonuses and once on stats alone, and the planner keeps whichever version scores higher, because a greedy pass can lock in an early milestone that blocks a better use of the same Mithril.

    In Expedition mode the planner never stops at R140 or R180, since those imbuements only give Conquest bonuses; it still pays them on the way to R160 or R200. In Conquest mode it's the reverse: R140 and R180 count, and it won't pay for R120, R160 or R200 just for their troop bonuses. Hero bonuses get a neutral weight of 1 per %, since they help every troop type.
