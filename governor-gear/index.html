    How the order is chosen

    Each pick is the purchase, or short group of purchases, with the best weighted gain per material spent. A piece's % counts twice, for the Attack and the Defense of its troop type, times that troop's weight. Set bonuses help every troop type, so they count once per troop. Materials are weighted by scarcity: each one costs (amount used × amount you started with ÷ amount left²), so a material that is running low counts for more.

    To avoid stopping just short of a set bonus, the planner also looks ahead: it tries finishing the current level, the level after, and raising the pieces needed for the next 3-piece and 6-piece set bonus together.
